### Main results
1. Rebut the  "The Illusion of Thinking" paper from Apple

	2. Reasoning models today are not perfect, but that does not mean they cannot reason at all.
2. *Reflection on human intelligence*
	1. How it draws inspiration from nature but not exactly duplicates it. Plane vs flapping wings $\Leftrightarrow$ AI reasoner vs human reasoning
	2. Subjective experience makes reasoning a harder problem than physical tasks like flight
		1. Our mind manipulates; free will is one of those (useful) manipulations.
	3. "awareness" of reasoning model
		1. "Hallucinations are a great example of the type of complete awareness our AI systems lack."
		2. "To be self-aware is to be able to predict accurately with an understanding of what it is and what its environment is."
		3. Ilya’s provocation for why next-token prediction is enough "Predicting the next token well means that you understand the underlying reality that led to the creation of that token."
